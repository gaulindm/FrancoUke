ChordPro cheat sheet

Tags go on their own line, between curly braces. Click a title to open it.

{# ---------------- HEADER ---------------- #}

The more of these you fill in, the better the header looks, in the PDF and in the teleprompter. A line is left out when it has nothing to show.

title (or t)The song title, centre of the top row. The tag only fills in the title when the song has no title yet
timeSignatureTop row, left, for example 4/4
strummingTop row, right. Use D (down), U (up), X (mute), a dot for a rest and a dash between beat groups. A capital letter is a strong beat, a small letter a light beat, for example D-du-udu
1stnoteSecond row, left: "1st vocal note"
songwriterSecond row, centre
short_instruction_1Second row, right. If empty and the song has a slash chord, "(/ = one strum)" is shown
count_inThird row, left: "Count in"
artist, year, capoThird row, centre: "Matches the recording by …". With a capo number it reads "Capo (2) to match the recording by …", and {capo: based} gives "Based on clip by …". Needs an artist. The year must be four digits
short_instruction_2Third row, right
keyOverrides the key that is detected automatically from the chords (used when songs are shown or filtered by key)
tempo, albumKept with the song, but not shown in the header

How to use it: paste the tags at the top of the song, then fill in the ones you need. Tags left empty are simply ignored.

{artist: The Beatles}
{year: 1967}
{capo: 2}
{count_in: 1 2 3 4}
{# ---------------- SECTIONS ---------------- #}

Start a section with the first tag and end it with the second. Write each tag exactly like this, with nothing after the name.

{soi} … {eoi}Intro
{sov} … {eov}Verse (Couplet in French). A verse has no label in the PDF
{soc} … {eoc}Chorus (Refrain in French)
{sob} … {eob}Bridge (Pont in French)
{sod} … {eod}Interlude
{soo} … {eoo}Outro
{sos} … {eos}Centered (no label)
{page}Starts a new page in the PDF
{sot} … {eot}Tab lines, shown in a grey box

Intro, Chorus, Bridge, Outro and Interlude get a label in the margin of the PDF. Verse and Centered do not.

{instruction: ...} adds a small grey italic note under that label, for example "Play softly". Because a verse has no label, the note is not shown on a verse.

{# ---------------- COMMENTS ---------------- #}

{c: ...} and {comment: ...} are the same tag. What you see depends on what is inside:

{c: Slow down}
on its own line		 A red italic note on its own line
Hello {c: softly}
inside a lyric line		 A small grey italic note in the line
{c:(234)} A count cue at the end of the line. Only digits, spaces and commas inside the brackets
{c:(Two Parts)} Any other words in brackets stay an ordinary note
{c:VG1}
{c:VG2}		 Puts "Vocal Group 1:" or "Vocal Group 2:" in front of the next sung line ({c:1} and {c:2} also work)
{# ---------------- STYLES ---------------- #}

Put the tags around the words you want to change. They work in lyrics and in notes.

<b>word</b> or <strong>Bold
<i>word</i> or <em>Italic
<u>word</u>Underline
<red>word</red>Colour. Also: grey, blue, green, yellow, orange, pink, purple
<r> <g> <y>Short forms for red, green and yellow
<h>word</h>Yellow highlight
<highlight color="pink">word</highlight>Highlight in another colour

Colour and highlight tags are applied in the PDF.

{# ---------------- OPTIONAL CHORDS ---------------- #}

Put a chord in square brackets, right before the word or syllable where it changes:

[C]Hello, [G]world

Optional chords (ones players may leave out) go inside <opt> tags. They are printed in grey italic:

<opt>[Em]</opt>
{# ---------------- YOUTUBE ---------------- #}

The youtube tag is what drives the video players. Without it, there is no video.

Paste the full YouTube link. It must start with https://. To start the video part-way, add the time to the link, for example ?t=90 or ?t=1m30s.

{youtube: https://youtu.be/XXXXXXXXXXX?t=1m30s}
